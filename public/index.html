Packing Rule Cards

A traveler wants a tiny script explaining what to pack for three fictional weather conditions.

Choose a fictional forecast

Choose inputs, then make a card.

Rules you can inspect

Below 10°C: warm layer. From 10°C up to 25°C: light layer. At 25°C and above: sun hat. Every card includes a water bottle. Rain and wind each add their own item.

A branch-practice toy, not weather or travel safety advice.
